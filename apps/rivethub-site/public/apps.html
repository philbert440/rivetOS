Apps

The RivetHub apps.

The apps are how you see and steer your nodes: sessions, topics, terminals and memory. They talk to an rh node over its gateway; the agents keep running on your machines.

Don’t have a node yet? Start with the Quickstart →

Mobile

On your phone.

▯Build from source

Android

RivetHub for Android lives in the rivetOS repository as apps/rivethub-android; build it with Gradle (JDK 21 and the Android SDK). Pair it from the desktop app’s Settings → Devices → Pair a phone, then Scan pairing QR on the phone. Pairing needs a node with a certificate authority. Off your home network, connect over Tailscale.

Plug-ins

Add to your nodes.

A plug-in is its own program on a node: rh starts it, shows its views in the apps, sends it events and offers its tools to your agents. rivethub-plugins has the TypeScript and Rust SDKs, templates to start from, and the voice, files and Workflows plug-ins. An agent can also write one for you with the plug-in creator tools that rh serves over MCP. The desktop lists each node’s plug-ins under Settings → Plug-ins.
